1. Introduction

Welcome to Garden Puzzles ("we", "our", or " the App"), package ID com.gardenpuzzles.game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and how you can permanently delete your account and data at any time.

2. Information We Collect and Why

  • Account & Profile Information: You may play as a Guest (via anonymous authentication) or sign in using Google Sign-In. We store your chosen display name, optional profile avatar/photo, and selected country flag code so they appear on the Global Leaderboard.
  • Gameplay Progress Data: We store your current level, completed stages count, cumulative solve time, streak, and remaining Hints/Magic Wands in Google Firebase Firestore and locally on your device so your progress is never lost across sessions.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob & Firebase)

The App integrates trusted third-party services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display interstitial ads (after every 10 completed stages) and optional rewarded ads (to earn free Hints or Magic Wands). AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and coarse diagnostic data to serve and measure ads in compliance with Google Play and Amazon Appstore policies.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Used solely for secure user authentication, cloud progress backup, and the Global Top 10 Leaderboard.

4. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have full control over your data and can permanently delete your account and all associated Firebase records at any time through either of the following methods:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: Open Garden Puzzles → tap the Settings (Gear/Pause) icon → tap the red "Delete Account & Data" button → confirm deletion. This immediately and permanently erases your profile document from Cloud Firestore, deletes your Firebase Authentication account, and clears local device data.
  • Deletion by Email Request: You may also request complete account and data deletion by emailing us at mohanedshabbu8@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy & Data Security

All communication between the App and Google Firebase/AdMob servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell or share personal user data with any unauthorized third parties.
